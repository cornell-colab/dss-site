## Tools for collections as data work
Welcome to the platforms & tools page! Discover your new favourite way to make an engaging digital collection, database, visualization, map, or other public-facing piece of digital scholarship through the list of tools available below. This list is by no means exhaustive, and prioritizes open-source, free tools when available. If you notice that your favourite tool is missing, email Kiran at kam535@cornell.edu.

### Tool Types
#### Exhibit
An exhibit is a presentation of works of art, documents, objects, etc. alongside text or multimedia analysing, connecting, or otherwise considering those objects. An exhibit differs from a collection in that it is a curated subset of objects alongside some form of dedicated analysis, not just the entire collection at once.
#### Mapping
A mapping tool allows for the visual presentation of geospatial data (coordinate points, features, geographical shapes, etc.) defined in two-dimensional or three-dimensional space.
#### Publishing
A publishing tool is used primarily for creating structured text-based works, such as digital editions, books, journals, and magazines. Publishing tools support one or multiple parts of the publication process—from writing to editing to distribution.
#### Web-building
A web-building tool supports the creation, design, and deployment of websites.
#### Data cleaning
A data cleaning tool is used to import a dataset and rearrange it for machine-readability, clarity, or data validation.
#### Collection
A collection is a curated group of works of art, documents, objects, etc. with assigned and accessible metadata. A collection differs from an exhibit in that it does not explicitly provide accompanying text or analysis for the included items.
#### Data visualization
A data visualization tool is used to create visual representations of quantitative or qualitative data.
#### Other
Tools that do not fit into a larger existing category, such as code playgrounds and sandboxes, interactive web snippets, browser extensions, game design platforms, etc.
